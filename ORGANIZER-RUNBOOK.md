# Organizer Runbook

A lightweight checklist for the Agent Builders Club Build-a-thon.

## Before doors open

- [ ] Confirm venue access, tables, power, Wi-Fi, and display/projector.
- [ ] Have the event repository link ready.
- [ ] Have the prompt list ready to display or print.
- [ ] Prepare a simple team sign-up method.
- [ ] Confirm who will welcome people, keep time, and help with GitHub/tools.
- [ ] Open the demo screen and test it.
- [ ] Remind participants not to commit secrets or private data.

## Run of show

### 6:30 — Arrival

Welcome people, help them find seats, and invite them to meet others.

### 6:45 — Welcome and format

Explain the goal, event repository, team size, prompt process, prompt swap, and demos.

### 6:55 — Teams and prompts

Help people form teams of 2–4. Let each team choose or draw a prompt.

### 7:05 — Prompt swap window

Offer one optional prompt swap. Teams may keep their original prompt.

### 7:15 — Build time

Circulate, answer questions, help teams narrow scope, and remind them that a small complete result is enough.

### 8:25 — Demo preparation

Ask teams to finish their project README, commit their work, choose a presenter, and prepare a backup screenshot or recording.

### 8:40 — Demos

Give each team about three minutes. Keep the pace friendly and move on when time is up.

### 8:55 — Recognition and close

Call out standout projects, thank the venue and participants, explain the sharing-permission process, and take a group photo if people agree.

## Organizer rules of thumb

- Help teams reduce scope instead of adding features.
- Ask, “What can you show in the demo?”
- Treat unfinished work and useful failures as valid.
- Do not publish a project, screenshot, or participant information without permission.
- Never ask participants to paste API keys into chat or commit them to GitHub.
- If a team is blocked, help them create a smaller version they can demonstrate.

## After the event

- [ ] Confirm each project has a README and commit.
- [ ] Update the project index.
- [ ] Record demo links or videos where teams provide them.
- [ ] Record each team's sharing permission.
- [ ] Send a thank-you and follow-up message.
- [ ] Ask what should change before the next event.
- [ ] Share only projects that have explicit permission.
